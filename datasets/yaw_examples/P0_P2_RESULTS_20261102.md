## P1 — image frontend, visual residual and clamp attribution

The exact source C++ `GeminiEisProcessLiveYPlane` path received decoded 2560×1440 video (internally downsampling to 320×180). Approximately 293/294 valid frames per case applied a visual field. All 4 cases below had **zero** baseline activation of:
- Downstream `breathScale`
- `alphaSafe`
- Final normalized-UV vertex boundary clamp
- Second-gate virtual row targets
- Zoom 1.14

However, we instrumented the distinct earlier `kMaxDeltaUv=0.0042` **per-vertex vector-magnitude clamp**, which truncates the Jacobian-projected visual correction **before temporal smoothing**.

| Case | Original per-vertex magnitude-clamp frames / 294 | P95 affected vertices (out of 660) | A2 magnitude-clamp frames | A2 Breath frames |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 024605 | 283 | 660 | 0 | 0 |
| 024618 | 281 | 660 | 0 | 0 |
| 024630 | 236 | 660 | 0 | 4 |
| 024647 | 272 | 660 | 0 | 2 |

**A2:** uses the SAME golden core trajectory and KLT path; the *host-only* magnitude cap is raised to `0.5 UV` through an exported C++ setter. We confirmed the actual DLL cap is 0.5 and **zero** vertices were magnitude-capped. All four qTarget+Zoom SHA256 hashes match baseline exactly.

- This earlier magnitude clamp is **a frequently active mechanism**. It must not be confused with the never-active **final vertex boundary clamp**.
- Removing it increases visual correction from roughly 8–11 to roughly 22–37 source-pixel RMS P95 at the edges, and occasionally begins to activate `breathScale`.
- The resulting spatial field is **not uniformly better** after removing best 2D similarity; on 024630 right edge P95 non-similarity residual rises from **0.584 to 0.760 input pixels**, whereas other cases may decrease.
- These residual numbers isolate the *additional visual field*; they do **not** measure overall real-world edge stretch caused by K/timing errors, wide FOV or parallax.

### Independent left/right versus center differential

To avoid confusing center-only stability with side deformation, a second measurement was performed on the final **1920×1080 four-way H.264 video**, tracking 293 transitions independently per quadrant. Each quadrant was 960×540. Per-region motion was estimated with fresh image KLT; relative horizontal flow subtracts the center region's median.

| Metric, absolute P95 across transitions | Golden | A2 unclamped | No visual |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Left minus center horizontal displacement | **1.620 px** | 1.786 px | 1.811 px |
| Right minus center horizontal displacement | **1.505 px** | 1.819 px | 1.411 px |
| Left minus center local horizontal affine strain (proxy) | 4.18% | 5.56% | 3.97% |
| Right minus center local horizontal affine strain (proxy) | 2.77% | 2.38% | 2.82% |

**Caveat:** These are robustly estimated *frame-to-frame local affine flow proxies*, not calibrated world-static strain. Translation, parallax, moving subjects, camera projection, line structure, feature replacement and compression remain confounders. The left/right strain comparison is **mixed**, and does not support claiming A2 fixed peripheral deformation.

### Interpretation

The Opus "boundary-pressure cluster" is **not active** in this test apart from **four initial orientation-releases** on the two large-yaw sequences. The newly identified **upstream 0.0042 UV magnitude cap is active frequently**, but its removal does **not improve the 024630 frozen-trajectory pixel/edge scores**. Therefore **do not ship A2**. The fundamental yaw edge stretch is still unresolved.

Highest-value remaining investigation: **actual post-ISP optical/geometry calibration** (fx/fy/cx/cy/k1/k2 and whether the ISP already corrected distortion), **gyro-to-camera extrinsic**, **readout timing / +4ms phase alignment**, and actual **GPU / camera buffer ROI**. Under correct analytic 3D rotation, distinguish natural rectilinear magnification, parallax and source FOV limitations from model-induced temporal edge deformation.
